# ASTRA: Scientific Bibliography & Reference Documentation

The clinical emergency workflows, physiological thresholds, and spaceflight hazard definitions in ASTRA are informed by peer-reviewed aerospace medicine research, spaceflight clinical practice standards, and bioastronautics literature.

---

## 1. Spaceflight Hazards & Human Health
1. **NASA Human Research Program**: *Human Research Roadmap: Evidence Reports on Human Health Risks of Space Exploration*. National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA), Houston, TX.
2. **Cucinotta, F. A., & Durante, M. (2006)**: *Cancer risk from exposure to galactic cosmic rays: implications for early exploration missions*. The Lancet Oncology, 7(5), 431-435.
3. **Crucian, B. E., et al. (2018)**: *Immune system dysregulation during spaceflight: potential countermeasures for deep space exploration missions*. Frontiers in Immunology, 9, 1437.
4. **Patel, Z. S., et al. (2020)**: *Red risks for a journey to the red planet: The highest priority human health risks for a mission to Mars*. npj Microgravity, 6(1), 33.

## 2. Spaceflight-Associated Neuro-Ocular Syndrome (SANS)
5. **Lee, A. G., et al. (2020)**: *Spaceflight-associated neuro-ocular syndrome (SANS)*. Eye, 34(6), 1019-1033.
6. **Mader, T. H., et al. (2011)**: *Optic disc edema, globe flattening, choroidal folds, and hyperopic shifts observed in astronauts after long-duration space flight*. Ophthalmology, 118(10), 2058-2069.
7. **Stenger, M. B., et al. (2017)**: *Risk of spaceflight-associated neuro-ocular syndrome (SANS)*. NASA Evidence Report.

## 3. Neuro-Vestibular Adaptation & Sensorimotor Coordination
8. **Reschke, M. F., et al. (1998)**: *Posture and locomotion: sensorimotor adaptation to altered gravity*. Journal of Gravitational Physiology, 5(1), P9-P12.
9. **Clément, G., & Reschke, M. F. (2008)**: *Neuroscience in Space*. Springer Science & Business Media, New York.
10. **Kornilova, L. N., et al. (2012)**: *Vestibular dysfunction and gaze tracking anomalies after long-duration orbital spaceflight*. Human Physiology, 38(2), 140-149.

## 4. Computer Vision & Non-Invasive Facial Screening
11. **Soukupova, T., & Cech, J. (2016)**: *Real-Time Eye Blink Detection using Facial Landmarks*. Computer Vision Winter Workshop (CVWW), Rimske Toplice, Slovenia.
12. **Verkruysse, W., Svaasand, L. O., & Nelson, J. S. (2008)**: *Remote plethysmographic imaging using ambient light*. Optics Express, 16(26), 21434-21445.
13. **McDuff, D., et al. (2014)**: *Remote measurement of cognitive stress via photoplethysmographic imaging of autonomic nervous system response*. IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering, 61(10), 2636-2644.

## 5. Aerospace Emergency Medicine & Life Support
