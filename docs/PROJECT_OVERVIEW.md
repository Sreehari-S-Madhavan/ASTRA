# ASTRA: Project Overview

## 1. Mission & Objective
The **Astronaut Health & Safety Monitoring System (ASTRA)** is designed to support the medical autonomy, safety, and health maintenance of astronauts during long-duration deep space missions. During exploratory missions to the Moon, Mars, or deep-space transit vehicles, direct real-time communication with Earth-based flight surgeons is prohibited by physical light-time radio delays ranging from minutes to tens of minutes.

ASTRA functions as an onboard, offline-capable medical workstation that combines continuous physiological monitoring, computer vision-based visual screening, spaceflight hazard assessment, transparent risk evaluation, emergency decision support, and remote physician communication.

## 2. Originality & Branding Requirements
- **Original Identity**: ASTRA features an independent, original visual design and brand architecture. It contains no third-party space agency logos, trademarks, or copyrighted assets.
- **Custom Character**: The virtual companion **Astraea** is a custom, procedural vector-rendered celestial entity with 7 dynamic expressive states.
- **Independent Clinical Framework**: Clinical emergency workflows are based on established open aerospace medicine principles paraphrased from scientific literature, with explicit disclaimers distinguishing visual observations from clinical diagnostic procedures.

## 3. Core Capabilities Summary
| Operational Pillar | Implemented Capabilities |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Camera-Based Screening** | Real-time face detection, eye tracking, EAR blink rate, head pose projection, guided visual pursuit tests, reaction-time measurement, and camera quality assessment. |
| **Physiological Monitoring** | Real-time monitoring of Heart Rate, SpO2, Blood Pressure, Core Temperature, Respiratory Rate, and Hydration Index with safe-range graphical shading. |
| **Spaceflight Hazards** | Continuous monitoring of Radiation, Isolation/Fatigue, Distance/Latency, Microgravity vestibular adaptation, and ECLSS Closed Environment telemetry. |
| **Risk Assessment** | Rule-based multi-factor evaluation engine with transparent indicator breakdowns, missing data tracking, and three operational alert levels (`LOW`, `MODERATE`, `CRITICAL`). |
| **Emergency Protocols** | Zero-navigation access to 10 clinical emergency protocols with step-by-step checklists, contraindication warnings, and automatic Flight Surgeon escalation. |
| **Physician Communication** | Deep space communication simulator with configurable light-time delay, store-and-forward message queues, offline autonomy, and Flight Surgeon acknowledgement. |
| **Reporting & Export** | High-resolution PDF medical dossier generation featuring telemetry tables, screening results, risk breakdowns, and digital sign-off blocks. |
| **Mission Simulation** | Integrated simulation engine with 12 anomaly scenarios, manual parameter sliders, and adjustable speed scaling. |
